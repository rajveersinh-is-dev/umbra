## 2. Audit Summary & Methodological Findings

1. **Epistemic Honesty**: Zero unsupported or unprovable claims remain in the active codebase.
2. **Router Nature**: The Auto Router is an empirical **risk-mitigation policy**, not an omniscient classifier. It prevents catastrophic degradation (e.g. Heckman cell RMSE exploding to 5.96 on MCAR).
3. **Inference Guardrails**: Second-stage Heckman standard errors are clearly qualified as approximate unadjusted OLS standard errors; multiple imputation pooling via Rubin's rules is provided and formally tested.
