# Plan 03-02: RubinPooler & Barnard-Rubin (1999) Small-Sample Degrees of Freedom

## Goal
Implement a dedicated `RubinPooler` class and enhance `rubins_rules()` with Barnard & Rubin (1999) small-sample adjusted degrees of freedom ($\nu_{\text{adj}}$), Fraction of Missing Information (FMI), and downstream model pooling capabilities.

      - `RubinsRulesResult` (dataclass with `pooled_estimate`, `within_variance`, `between_variance`, `total_variance`, `standard_error`, `df`, `df_adjusted`, `df_complete`, `fmi`, `relative_variance`, `relative_efficiency`, `ci_lower`, `ci_upper`, `p_value`).
      - `rubins_rules(point_estimates, variance_estimates, df_complete=None, alpha=0.05)` implementing:
        - Rubin (1987) variance decomposition: $T = \bar{U} + (1 + 1/M)B$.
        - Barnard-Rubin (1999) small-sample correction when `df_complete` is provided: $\nu_{\text{adj}} = \frac{\nu_m \nu_{\text{obs}}}{\nu_m + \nu_{\text{obs}}} \le \nu_0$.
        - FMI $\hat{\lambda}$ and relative efficiency $\text{RE} = (1 + \hat{\lambda}/M)^{-1}$.
      - `RubinPooler` class:
        - `pool_estimates(point_estimates, variance_estimates, df_complete=None, alpha=0.05)`: pools scalar or vector arrays.
        - `fit(imputed_dfs, target, feature_cols=None, estimator=None)`: fits downstream estimator (default: OLS linear regression) across $M$ imputed DataFrames and pools parameters.
        - Exposes fitted properties: `coef_`, `intercept_`, `stderr_`, `pvalues_`, `ci_`, `fmi_`, `df_`.
    - `umbra/imputers/mar_chained_equations.py` re-exports `rubins_rules` and `RubinsRulesResult` from `umbra.imputers.rubin_pooler` for backward compatibility.
    - `RubinPooler` and `RubinsRulesResult` exported in `umbra` and `umbra.imputers`.
