# Plan 06-02: Identifiability & Assumption Audit Certificates

**Goal:** Implement `IdentifiabilityCertificate` and `audit_identifiability` in `umbra/diagnostics/identifiability_audit.py`, integrate with `UmbraDiagnosticReport`, and author the Phase 6 test suite.
**Requirements:** `IDENT-02`

## Tasks

1. **Create `umbra/diagnostics/identifiability_audit.py`**:
   - Class `IdentifiabilityCertificate`:
     - `feature: str`
     - `missing_rate: float`
     - `manski_bounds: ManskiBoundsResult`
     - `testable_evidence: Dict[str, Any]` (Little's p-value, max Cohen's d, F-stat, tail correlation)
     - `strategy_assumptions: Dict[str, Dict[str, Any]]` (MICE/MAR, Heckman, Pattern Mixture, Manski)
     - `audit_verdict: str`
     - `scientific_disclaimer: str`

   - Function `audit_identifiability(col, df, risk_report=None, shadow_var=None, support=None) -> IdentifiabilityCertificate`.
2. **Integrate with `UmbraDiagnosticReport` and `diagnose_report`**:
   - Extend `UmbraDiagnosticReport` to include `identifiability_certificates: Dict[str, IdentifiabilityCertificate]`.
   - Update `diagnose_report` in `umbra/api.py` to compute and attach certificates for incomplete columns.
   - Update `diagnostics_to_markdown` in `umbra/explain.py` to render the audit certificate section.
3. **Re-export and Author Tests**:

   - Author `tests/test_identifiability_and_manski.py`.
